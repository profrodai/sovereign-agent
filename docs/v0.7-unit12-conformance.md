# v0.7 Unit 12 — adversarial conformance

A shared suite runs against Docker, Podman, loopback SSH, and `FaultWorker`:
stale tokens, forged completion, replay, disconnect, crashes, disk full,
timeout, cancellation, corruption, secret leakage, and network escape.

Sustained mixed workloads require at least three workers and two hosts. No
execution finalizes twice. No resource or secret lease survives its bound.
Skip-if-missing is not a pass for a claimed backend.
