# v0.7 Unit 10 — reconciliation and recovery

`ReconciliationEngine` compares desired state with worker observation after
restart or disconnect. `unknown` is explicit. Retry safety is declared, never
inferred. Late or duplicate completions are quarantined. There is no
last-write-wins finalization. Reassignment requires fencing and a side-effect
safety proof. One canonical receipt is preserved; contradictory diagnostics are
linked, not substituted.
